# Forensic Evidence

**Movement**: Forensic Evidence

Data is not neutral. It leaves marks — traces of what happened behind closed doors that the naked eye misread. This movement treats statistical truth as crime scene photography: clinical, precise, and quietly devastating. The composition arranges evidence on a dark field, each fragment demanding to be read against its neighbor, revealing a gap between perception and fact.

**Space and Form**: The canvas is a dossier. Negative space is interrogation — silence that gives weight to each data point. Information lives in vertical columns and horizontal bands that evoke classified reports, building dread through accumulation. Nothing decorates; everything testifies. Forms are geometric and deliberate, arranged with the meticulousness of an expert who has labeled every exhibit.

**Color and Material**: A forensic palette: deep obsidian backgrounds absorb light like a sealed room. A single saturated gold cuts through as primary accusation. Red and green appear only as verdict — minimal, loaded, never decorative. Gray is the neutral bureaucratic field where facts are placed without mercy. Every color choice is the product of painstaking calibration; nothing is accidental.

**Scale and Rhythm**: Scale communicates hierarchy of evidence. The most damning number dominates — enormous, unavoidable. Supporting facts are rendered in precise, small type, like annotations on a laboratory report. The rhythm is deliberate: silence, then impact, then documentation, then silence again. The eye is led through the evidence in sequence, arriving at the conclusion the designer has already reached.

**Composition and Balance**: Asymmetric tension, resolved by structural logic rather than symmetry. The composition feels like it could only have been assembled this way — every element placed with the care of someone who spent countless hours considering each relationship. Typography is sparse: bold declarations, whispered labels, nothing in between. The result looks as though it required weeks of labor to achieve its apparent simplicity — a masterwork of restraint from someone at the absolute top of their field.
