# Tactical Cartography

**Tactical Cartography** treats statistical contribution not as numbers but as territory — each athlete's share of a collective action mapped like land claims on an expedition chart. The philosophy borrows from the visual grammar of geological surveys and military reconnaissance: precise, analytical, yet quietly dramatic. Every bar is a measured boundary; every percentage is a coordinate on a map of collective effort.

## Space and Form

Space is divided with territorial rigor. Six equal provinces, each governing a single dimension of play. Within each province, ten horizontal stripes — one per player — assert their dominion over that metric. The width of the stripe is law: it admits no decoration, only proportion. Negative space is as load-bearing as the marks themselves; the silence between bars carries the weight of players who contributed less.

## Color and Material

A single chromatic axis: deep obsidian ground against which gold ignites. The palette is monastic in its restraint — one hue, many depths. The top contributor burns at full intensity; all others dim proportionally, like stars at varying distances. This creates an immediate visual hierarchy without a single explanatory word: brightness encodes dominance.

## Scale and Rhythm

The six provinces repeat with the cadence of a musical phrase — identical in structure, distinct in melody. A reader's eye learns the grammar once and reads six panels fluently. The horizontal bar is the visual atom: atomic, unadorned, metrically precise. Its length is the only variable that speaks.

## Composition and Balance

Two rows of three panels sit beneath a commanding header that anchors the composition. The header carries identity — badge, title, temporal scope — then yields entirely to the data. No decorative elements compete. Typography is clinical: a thin, cool label system that refuses to shout. The footer whispers attribution.

## Craftsmanship

This is the product of meticulous attention — every padding value calibrated, every font weight deliberate, every color opacity mathematically derived from contribution ratios. The work must appear as though it took countless hours to compose: the spacing between bar and label chosen with the care of a cartographer placing a legend marker, the panel separators as precise as surveyor's lines. A master-level execution that rewards sustained examination.
