# Thermal Glow

**Thermal Glow** treats athletic performance not as numbers but as radiated energy — each data point a cell of heat, each composition an infrared scan of collective effort. The philosophy draws from scientific imaging and dark-room photography: forms emerge from darkness through the precise application of light, not through decoration. Every element earns its luminosity.

## Color as Thermal Gradient

The palette is non-negotiable: absolute darkness anchors the zero point, and contribution generates warmth. Near-black (#0f0f12) is the absence of output; deep amber (#7a5200) marks moderate presence; full gold (#F5C400) signals dominance. This is not decoration — it is a temperature reading. The gradient must feel physically real, as though the card radiates heat at its brightest points. Meticulous calibration of this spectrum separates craft from accident.

## Form and Density

Identical circular forms repeat in a strict grid — modules of information arranged like cells under a microscope. No circle varies in size; only temperature differentiates. The discipline is absolute. Negative space between modules is load-bearing silence: it sharpens the contrast between hot and cold cells, making the heat map legible in a single glance. This restraint is the product of painstaking compositional judgment.

## Hierarchy Through Luminosity

Visual hierarchy emerges entirely from brightness, not from scale or decoration. The brightest cell in each column is the focal point — no labels, annotations, or badges are needed when the color speaks. A thin luminous ring marks the peak value: a halo, not a trophy. The eye moves naturally toward heat, then reads outward into context. This is master-level information design — the kind that takes countless hours to calibrate correctly.

## Typography as Clinical Instrument

Text is sparse, precise, and functional — numbers and names only. Rank numerals anchor the left column in large, low-contrast gold, like catalog indices. Player names carry weight through confidence: bold, white, unhurried. Metric headers are yellow caps, exact and brief. Every typographic decision is made as if it were the last one available — nothing added, nothing removed. The result should feel as though a master typographer spent days on spacing alone.

## Spatial Rhythm and Craft

The composition breathes through consistent rhythm: equal spacing between all modules, an exact left margin for identity elements, a precise right boundary. Alternating row tones (imperceptible at distance, clarifying up close) create depth without decoration. The finished piece should look as though it required extraordinary effort to produce — an artifact that could exist in a gallery or a data center with equal credibility.
